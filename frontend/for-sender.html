For Sender

Gain Trust, Show Commitment. Here is what the sending side does on Tetscrow.

Open App How Tetscrow Works
1

Create the agreement

Enter the receiver's wallet address, split the payment into milestones, set a deadline, and add a link to the written terms.

2

Fund the escrow

Deposit the agreed amount. It is locked in the smart contract, so neither Tetscrow nor the receiver can touch it until the deal resolves.

3

Wait for delivery

Follow the agreement's status on your dashboard. Each milestone changes to Delivered when the receiver marks the work done. If you do nothing for 7 days after that, the receiver can claim the payment.

4

Review and release

Check the work against the terms. Release sends that milestone's USDC straight to the receiver's wallet. Releasing is final, so review first.

5

Dispute if needed

If the work does not match the agreement, raise a dispute before you release. The milestone's funds freeze and the platform arbitrator decides how they are split.
